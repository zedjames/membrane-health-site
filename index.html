Why "membrane"?

Everything alive has an edge.

A cell holds its shape with a membrane — a living boundary that flexes and stretches, but only so far. Your body works the same way. There's a limit to how much you can take and still bounce back.

Stay inside it and you adapt, recover, and grow. Push past it and you break down. Membrane Health finds your edge from your own data and shows you where you stand in relation to it — every single day. That distance is capacity: room to move, room to adapt, room to recover, room to keep being yourself under changing load.

— the light you find by going in.

Timing is everything

It's what your body is doing — and when.

Timing reaches into far more than clocks or schedules. It shapes whether the movements of life arrive in an order the body can actually receive, organize, and use well.

Your body is a web of rhythms that stay in time with one another, and when that timing slips you often feel it first, ahead of any single number. So Membrane Health reads your timing alongside your state: the order things arrive in, the window in the day when effort will land, how many days until you're recovered, and where you are in your own cycle. Timing is the axis everything here is built around.
