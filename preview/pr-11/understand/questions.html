From question to understanding

One question can involve several interacting processes.

A feeling, a number, and a day of activity are not the same kind of information. A careful explanation keeps them distinct, then asks how they might relate.

Membrane Health uses selected observations from Apple Health to construct a defined mathematical interpretation. Its results are not a diagnosis, and the reason for persistent symptoms cannot be established by wearable data alone.
