How the reading is constructed

What Does Membrane Health Measure—and What Does It Infer?

Every useful health interpretation should say what was directly observed, what was calculated, and what remains a claim to be tested. These layers have different kinds of evidence.

In brief

Every useful health interpretation should say what was directly observed, what was calculated, and what remains a claim to be tested. These layers have different kinds of evidence.

1. Observations: what is available

With your authorization, Apple Health can provide records such as sleep measures, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, and activity. Availability differs with devices, permissions, wear habits, and collection conditions. Some readings may be absent or irregular.

These are the measurement inputs. They are partial observations of physiology, not a complete recording of every process in the body.

2. Computation: what is derived

A reproducible calculation transforms declared inputs using specified mathematical rules. Membrane Health uses a personal baseline and defined relationships to interpret recent observations. When the inputs or rules change, the result can change too.

A derived index is a property of the recorded data and the model. It is important to know which inputs contributed and how missing observations were handled.

3. Interpretation: what the model says

Membrane Health presents readable summaries such as Go, Caution, and Recover. It also uses concepts including available capacity, load, timing, coherence, and recovery trajectory to explain the modeled position.

Those terms express interpretations within a defined framework. They should not be mistaken for direct laboratory measurements of all the corresponding biological mechanisms.

4. Formal mathematics: what is established

A formally specified mathematical system can be analyzed for consistency, defined properties, and consequences of its assumptions. Where individual results are machine-checked, those proofs pertain to the stated formal objects.

Formal verification does not by itself validate consumer-wearable accuracy, biological mechanisms, diagnostic performance, or clinical outcomes.

5. Empirical testing: what remains to be established

Physiological validation asks how model-derived readings behave in real people: whether they are repeatable under comparable conditions, whether they distinguish relevant states, and whether they predict meaningful outcomes when prediction is claimed.

Evidence for those questions must be evaluated in the relevant population and circumstances. Until demonstrated, a proposed physiological interpretation remains a research hypothesis or model-level description.

Your data and your decisions

Membrane Health is designed around authorized data access and user-controlled interpretation. The Privacy Policy explains data handling. A reading is intended to inform reflection, not diagnose conditions, replace professional evaluation, or make decisions for you.

For the scientific background, visit The Science, and for foundational definitions, explore health as capacity.
