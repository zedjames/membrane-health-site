The science

A living position,
built from measurement.

Membrane Health locates today's state within a formal definition of health and the history of your own physiology. The measurement begins with authorized signals, carries them through defined relationships, and resolves them into a position that can be followed as it moves through time.

Layer 1 · The human idea

Health is organized continuity under changing load.

A living system stays itself by organizing what arrives and preserving enough continuity for adaptation, exchange, and recovery to continue as conditions change.

A cell holds its shape through a membrane that regulates exchange across a living boundary, and our bodies express the same principle at larger scales of organization. Membrane Health reads how that organization is holding today and how much capacity remains within its current boundary.

Layer 2 · The mathematical object

A defined state, with an edge you can measure toward.

Underneath the daily reading is a formal state space with defined bounds, relationships that unfold through time, and a defined region of health.

State & bounds

Your state has a position. Your position has context.

Your reading is a position within that structure, calibrated to the history of your own physiology. Each reading locates today's state within the mathematical structure of health and within the movement of your own physiology through time.

Defined quantities

Coherence, recovery, capacity

Coherence, stability, recovery, and capacity become defined quantities within the state space, and their relationships give the position its shape and context.

A reading that can show its work.

  1. Reads the Apple Health measurements you authorize.
  2. Checks their quality and sufficiency.
  3. Calibrates the coordinates to your physiological history.
  4. Computes load, reserve, timing, recovery, and cross-system relationships.
  5. Locates the resulting state within the defined region.
  6. Go, Caution, or Recover — the simplest expression of that position, with the contributing measurements preserved alongside it.
