The way we approach health

Three layers of understanding.

01

The human question

Am I ready? Why did my recovery change? What does this number mean for my day?

02

The observations

Sleep, activity, resting heart rate, HRV, and other authorized measurements have scope and limitations.

03

The interpretation

Explicit calculations relate observed patterns to a personal baseline; validation tests what the resulting interpretation can support.

A living baseline

Your history gives each measurement context.

Membrane Health uses a rolling 28-day personal baseline. A number becomes more meaningful when we know how it relates to your own recent patterns, the conditions under which it was collected, and the direction of change.

The app organizes available observations into a defined mathematical reading. It does not directly measure every aspect of your physiology or replace clinical assessment.

Scientific transparency

We distinguish measurement, computation, mathematical proof, and empirical validation. A formal result about a model is not automatically a demonstrated claim about human physiology.
